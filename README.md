## What I build

- **HORIZON SHIELD**: buyer-side AI verification for construction and renovation estimates. It reads a quote, checks it against open cost data, and shows the reasoning so anyone can recompute the result.
- **JCCDB**: an open Japanese construction-cost database. CC BY 4.0, DOI-registered, evidence-linked. The public ground truth underneath the verification.
- **Verification Gate + JIDEC**: a conformance checker and a public, append-only verification ledger. You fetch the bytes, recompute the hash, check the record. No trust in me required.
- **hs-femtech-mcp**: the same idea applied to health information. A neutral registry that measures the transparency of femtech and health sources, never their medical truth. Peer review in progress.
- **Yakumo**: a verified contractor directory with zero referral fees. A listing is earned by passing the check, never bought.

All of it is built MCP-native, so an AI agent can call the verification and get a receipt instead of a marketing claim.

## How I verify

- **Recomputable.** Every verdict is reproducible from public bytes with SHA-256. Believe nothing, recompute everything.
- **Independent.** No referral fees, no pay-for-endorsement. A verifier that is paid to recommend is not a verifier.
- **Honest about limits.** Verified means untampered, not re-audited. When I do not know, I say I do not know. Fail-closed by default.
